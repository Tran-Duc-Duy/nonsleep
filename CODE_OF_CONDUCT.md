# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text at that link applies to all project spaces: issues,
pull requests, discussions, and any other channel where people represent the project.

In short:

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful; assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; focus on what is best for the community.
- No harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, sexualized language or imagery,
  or publishing others' private information.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email the maintainer at
[tranduybthv@gmail.com](mailto:tranduybthv@gmail.com). All reports are reviewed promptly and kept
confidential. Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors whose behavior is harmful, following the Covenant's
[enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
